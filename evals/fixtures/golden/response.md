name and cites generated service evidence. It models Choice, Lookup, and Person
as typed fields. The UI has loading, empty, and error states with accessible
labels. Authorization uses least privilege and read-only defaults. No direct
fetch or axios, invented fields, tenant IDs, or secrets are used.

final report.
