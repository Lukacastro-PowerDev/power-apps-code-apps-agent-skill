# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to creating a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include respectful communication, constructive feedback, and evidence-based discussion.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing sensitive information.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.
